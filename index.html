Core Concepts

Chart padhna seekhoge, guess nahi karoge

Course ke teen sabse important smart-money concepts — jo aap khud chart par pehchanna seekhoge.

Break of Structure (BOS)

Jab price purane swing high/low ko todkar band hoti hai — trend continuation ka pehla clue.

Order Block

Woh last down-candle zone jahan se bade players ne buying shuru ki — future mein price wapas yahin react karti hai.

Fair Value Gap (FVG)

Teen candles ke beech ka imbalance zone — price aksar isse "fill" karne wapas aati hai, entry ke liye useful.
