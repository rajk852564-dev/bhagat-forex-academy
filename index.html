🔰 Lesson 1 – Forex Trading Kya Hai?

📖 Lesson Explanation

Forex ka matlab Foreign Exchange hai. Is market mein ek currency ko doosri currency ke against trade kiya jata hai.

Currency Pair Example

EUR/USD → Euro vs US Dollar

XAU/USD → Gold vs US Dollar

📝 Step-by-Step

Step 1: Currency pair ko samjho.
Step 2: Base currency aur quote currency identify karo.
Step 3: Price upar ya neeche move hone ka analysis karo.
Step 4: Entry se pehle Stop Loss decide karo.

💡 Important

Trading mein profit guaranteed nahi hota. Risk management sabse important hai.

🕯️ Lesson 2 – Candlestick Basics

📖 Candlestick Kya Hai?

Candlestick ek specific time period mein price ka Open, High, Low aur Close dikhata hai.

🕯️ Candle Structure

Open → Start Price

High → Highest Price

Low → Lowest Price

Close → Ending Price

📝 Step-by-Step

Step 1: Candle ka body dekho.
Step 2: Upper aur lower wick dekho.
Step 3: Previous candles se comparison karo.
Step 4: Sirf ek candle par trade mat lo.

⚠️ Important

Candlestick confirmation ke saath market context bhi zaroor dekho.

📈 Lesson 3 – Market Trend

📖 Trend Kya Hai?

Market generally Uptrend, Downtrend ya Sideways condition mein move kar sakta hai.

Uptrend: Higher High + Higher Low.
Downtrend: Lower High + Lower Low.
Sideways: Price range mein move karta hai.

💡 Rule

Trend ke opposite trade lene se pehle strong confirmation zaroor dekho.

🎯 Lesson 4 – Support & Resistance

📖 Support & Resistance

Support wo area hai jahan price ko buying interest mil sakta hai. Resistance wo area hai jahan selling interest aa sakta hai.

Step 1: Previous highs aur lows mark karo.
Step 2: Important zones identify karo.
Step 3: Breakout ya rejection observe karo.
Step 4: Stop Loss define karo.

📖 Risk Management

Trading mein capital protection sabse important hai. Har trade mein limited risk rakhna seekho.

Step 1: Pehle Stop Loss decide karo.
Step 2: Position size calculate karo.

💥 Lesson 7 – Break of Structure (BOS)

📖 BOS Kya Hai?

Break of Structure tab kaha jata hai jab price important previous swing level ko decisively break karta hai.

Step 1: Important swing identify karo.
Step 2: Break ko observe karo.
Step 3: Candle close ka wait karo.
Step 4: Higher timeframe context check karo.
Step 5: Sirf BOS dekhkar immediately trade mat lo.

⚠️ Important

Har breakout successful nahi hota. Confirmation aur risk management zaroori hai.

🏦 Lesson 8 – Liquidity Basics

📖 Liquidity Kya Hai?

Liquidity ka simple matlab market mein orders ki availability hai. Important highs aur lows ke aas-paas liquidity ho sakti hai.

🔄 Lesson 9 – Liquidity Sweep

📖 Liquidity Sweep Kya Hai?

Jab price kisi important high ya low ko temporarily break karke wapas reverse hota hai, ise liquidity sweep ke context mein dekha ja sakta hai.

Step 1: Previous High/Low mark karo.
Step 2: Equal High/Low observe karo.
Step 3: Sweep ke baad price reaction dekho.
Step 4: Confirmation ke bina entry mat lo.

⚠️ Important
