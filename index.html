📦 Lesson 9 – Order Block Basics

📖 Order Block Kya Hai?

Order Block ek important price area hai jahan se strong buying ya selling move start ho sakta hai. Simple language mein, yeh woh area hai jahan se market mein strong move aane ki possibility hoti hai.

🟢 Bullish Order Block (Demand Zone)

Strong bullish move se pehle jo last bearish candle ya consolidation area hota hai, use potential Bullish Order Block ke roop mein observe kiya ja sakta hai.

🔴 Bearish Order Block (Supply Zone)

Strong bearish move se pehle jo last bullish candle ya consolidation area hota hai, use potential Bearish Order Block ke roop mein observe kiya ja sakta hai.

📝 Step-by-Step

Step 1: Strong impulsive move identify karo.
Step 2: Strong move se pehle relevant candle/price area observe karo.
Step 3: Higher timeframe trend aur market structure check karo.
Step 4: Price ke Order Block area mein wapas aane ka wait karo.
Step 5: BOS, price action aur candle confirmation ke bina blind entry mat lo.

🎯 Practice

XAU/USD (Gold) chart par 3 strong moves identify karo aur unke possible Order Block areas mark karo.

⚠️ Important

Sirf Order Block touch hone par immediately trade mat lo. Structure + BOS + confirmation + proper risk management ke saath hi analysis karo.
