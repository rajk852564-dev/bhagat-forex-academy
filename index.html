📈 Lesson 3 — Market Trend

📖 Trend Kya Hota Hai?

Market generally teen conditions mein ho sakta hai: Uptrend, Downtrend ya Range.

📈 Uptrend

Higher High → Higher Low → Higher High

📝 Step-by-Step

Step 1: Chart ka higher timeframe dekho.
Step 2: Previous High aur Low mark karo.
Step 3: Higher High + Higher Low ho to Uptrend.
Step 4: Lower High + Lower Low ho to Downtrend.
Step 5: Clear direction na ho to Range ho sakta hai.
